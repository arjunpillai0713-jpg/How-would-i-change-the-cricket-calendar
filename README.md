# How-would-i-change-the-cricket-calendar
This is my personal opinion and view on the cricket calendar should change
How would I change the cricket calendar?

Lets be honest cricket being overloaded , there’s a match every two days against teams playing each other again and again . 
Billaterlas :Bilaterlas dont make sense , there isnt a reason to watch it , It’s  basically a friendly game with no objective except winning a friendly cup.First i would remove bilaterals . It isn't exciting any more. It's been repeated a million times . It won't attract new cricket fans at all,remember ur most played song when u first heard it u loved it , after u kept hearing it again and again it doesn't give that feeling right? We need to understand that for something to be valuable it should not be repeated a thousand times .It should be precious not excess. We can still keep international international cricket interesting drawing inspiration from the test format itself we can launch a t20  world league with matches not series , india doesn’t play australia 3rd time in 7 days  , this keeps it interesting something like the top 4 teams play semi finals etc , if we do a series like 3matches to give points it makes us lose novelty something new every next match , it should be decided though draws , weaker teams should be given equal exposure . for odis we could do something like a tri series . Test  Cricket should remain as it is because it’s the purest form of the game .

Franchise cricket: franchise cricket has so many leagues now , mainly the IPL , which has indian players all of the other indian players cant play in any other league expect the ipl , now we should be focused on one league at a time we should not keep international matches between its too complicated. The BCCI should remove this rule and lift off the monopoly but is this practical NOO . it may be very hard to make bcci  remove this rule . Bcci earns 60% of its revenue through the ipl . This is a MAJOR problem since india is  the country for cricket culture and casual fans would not even watch international leagues. 

Commercialization of domestic cricket : Domestic cricket is very competitive actually , it's fun to watch but we don't see the quality in it like international matches , domestic cricket can be a great business strategy and fans would love it . We  should promote watching more domestic matches and not keep internationals during that time , making international matches more valuable and giving proper attention to them . we should build fan culture identity around it , rivalries etc . This will naturally increase the quality of domestic cricket . I am speaking not just for India, I'm saying globally this should be done.


More teams and equal chances in the world cups: first t20 wc should also be held every 4years.
All teams should be allowed to participate or all teams should play qualifiers instead of playing qualifying just to be the 2 weak teams of the wc . there should be random draws not putting india and pakistan and two associates in one group . Let it be competitive , this will increase viewership in small nations also gradually . 

These small changes may make a big difference in the long term and maybe make cricket more popular over time . This system is a tweaked system from the one we have now . It would take convincing to change it , it should be practical. But this is just my opinion or view on it , i have given thoughts to how practical a club based system would be but creating clubs etc would take a lot of time , making international leagues , the indian players and pakistan problem has not been considered here and that's a very big problem also cricket faces . Im not telling to be open to pak players its a serious safety concern for the nation and safety always come first giving this a thought , the franchise cricket may remain as it is but all the rest of changes will help cricket definitely
